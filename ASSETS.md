## Studio concept

Final asset: `public/images/studio-concept.webp` (1200px, optimized from the generated PNG). Created with the built-in **imagegen** tool, not the fallback CLI. The preview labels this as a studio concept; it is not represented as a photograph of an actual Apex newsroom.

Final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: photorealistic architectural concept background for a premium Indian TV news channel website broadcast preview. Wide 16:9 landscape empty modern broadcast newsroom studio with a sophisticated curved anchor desk, wall of subtle news video monitors, large dark world map LED wall, black and charcoal architecture, precise red illuminated trim, brushed metal and glass. Cinematic expensive broadcast lighting, dramatic controlled contrast, credible television studio, empty with no people. View from front slightly diagonal, medium-wide studio interior. Leave left third relatively uncluttered and dark for HTML headline overlay. High-end real architectural photography style, not sci-fi, not a UI screenshot. No typography, no text, no logos, no watermark. This is a fictional set concept, not documentary news photography.
